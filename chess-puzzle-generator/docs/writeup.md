## The hook (why this project)

Most puzzle trainers work by downloading a database of human-curated puzzles — they need an internet connection and someone else's curation work. I wanted to understand how those puzzles are found in the first place: can you write an algorithm that looks at a chess position and decides whether it's a good puzzle?
